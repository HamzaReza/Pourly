Delete your Pourly account

If you signed in to Pourly with Google, you can delete your account and the data stored with it here. You can also do it in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account and data.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save: levels, stars, coins, hints, undos, extra tubes, daily streaks and history, the daily gift cycle and unlocked themes.
  • Records of purchases delivered to your account by our server.
  • Your Pourly sign-in (the link between Pourly and your Google account).

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Purchases themselves are handled by Google Play: Remove Ads and VIP stay with your Google Play account and can be restored in the app, and subscriptions must be cancelled in Google Play.

Sign in with the Google account you use in Pourly to continue.
