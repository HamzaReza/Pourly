Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-25

This policy explains what information the Android game Pourly: Water Sort Puzzle ("Pourly", "the app", package com.pourly.water.app) collects, why, who receives it and the choices you have.

Developer and data controller: Infratek Systems
Contact (privacy and support): rezahamza5@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • You can play Pourly without an account. As a guest, your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, we store your account identifiers and a copy of your game progress in Google Firebase so it can follow you to other devices.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not collect push tokens.
  • Pourly has no analytics service and no crash-reporting service (no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics).
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data in the app or on our website at any time.

2. Information we collect and how we use it

2.1 Guest play (no account)

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, undos, extra tubes, daily challenge results and streaks, daily gift progress, unlocked themes, settings, and a list of purchase transaction ids already delivered) is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it.

The app also stores, on the device only: your settings (sound, haptics, colour-blind symbols, reduced motion, performance mode, language), notification preferences, ad pacing timestamps and review-prompt history.

2.2 Optional Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication)

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication (a Google service) to create your Pourly account. We receive and store:

  • your Firebase user id (a random identifier),
  • your email address,
  • your display name (and Firebase Authentication may also keep the profile photo link that Google provides; the app does not use it).

We use these only to sign you in, to show which account is signed in, to link your purchases to your account and to keep your cloud save. We do not send you marketing email.

2.3 Cloud save (Cloud Firestore)

While you are signed in, a copy of your game progress is stored in Cloud Firestore under your user id, so it can be restored on another device. The document contains:

schemaVersion, level, stars (stars per level), coins, hints, undos, extraTubes, streak, bestStreak, lastDailyDate, dailyHistory (your latest 120 daily results: stars and moves), giftDayIndex, lastGiftDate, ownedThemes, activeTheme, processedTransactions (up to 200 store order ids already delivered, used to avoid granting a purchase twice), starterPackPurchased, updatedAt (server time of the last save) and lastWriterInstall (a random id generated by the app for this installation; it is not a device or advertising id).

It never contains your entitlements, advertising data, notification settings or review history. Security rules allow only you (your signed-in account) to read or write your document.

2.4 Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The app uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and determine what you own (Remove Ads, VIP). RevenueCat receives your purchase history (product ids, order ids, purchase and expiry times, store receipts) and an app user id:

  • when you are signed in, the app user id is your Firebase user id;
  • otherwise it is an anonymous id generated by RevenueCat.

We do not send your email or name to RevenueCat.

Server purchase delivery. When you buy coins or the Starter Pack while signed in, RevenueCat notifies our server (a Google Cloud Function), which records the purchase in your account (users/{your id}/grants/{order id}: product id, order id, item amounts, the RevenueCat event id and type, environment, timestamps, whether it was applied and which installation applied it). The app then adds the items to your game. Refunds are recorded the same way so the items can be taken back. Purchases made as a guest are delivered on the device only.

2.5 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pourly shows rewarded ads (you choose to watch them for a reward) and interstitial ads (full-screen, between levels, with strict frequency limits). Ads are not shown to VIP members, and interstitials are not shown after you buy Remove Ads. Ads start only after you reach level 5.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google collects and processes, under its own policies, your device's advertising ID (and app set ID), device information (model, OS version, language, IP address and the approximate location derived from it), and usage and diagnostic data about ad interactions (impressions, clicks, ad performance and crashes in the ad SDK), for showing ads, measurement, fraud prevention and, where you allow it, personalised ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your consent before any ad request. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options (shown when it applies to you).
  • Content rating: ad requests are limited to a maximum content rating of PG, and are not tagged as directed at children.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your device).

2.6 Notifications

Reminders (daily challenge, streak, daily gift and "come back" reminders) are local notifications scheduled by the app on your device. They are computed on the device, no push service is used and we collect no push tokens. We ask for permission only after you have won a few levels, and you can turn reminders off in Settings or in Android settings.

2.7 What we do not collect

No analytics service, no crash-reporting service, no precise location, no contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, no in-app chat and no user-to-user features. The app's internal event log runs only in development builds and is never sent anywhere.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Contract (providing the service you ask for): cloud save, account sign-in, delivering and restoring purchases.
  • Consent: personalised advertising and the storage or access of information on your device for advertising (collected through Google's consent form). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Legitimate interests: non-personalised advertising that funds the free game, preventing fraud and duplicate purchase grants, keeping the service secure.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase and tax records where the law requires it.

4. Who receives your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that run parts of the app for us, and with Google as an advertising partner:

Recipient What Why
Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase Hosting) Account identifiers, cloud save, purchase grants Sign-in, cloud save, purchase delivery, account deletion
RevenueCat, Inc. App user id, purchase history Validating and restoring purchases
Google Play Purchases (Google is the payment processor) Payments and subscriptions
Google AdMob (and ad partners selected by Google, where you consent) Advertising ID, device and usage data Ads, measurement, fraud prevention

5. International transfers

Our service providers (Google and RevenueCat) process data in the United States and other countries, which may have different data-protection laws than yours. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the providers' data-processing terms. Cloud save data is stored in Cloud Firestore in the United States (nam5 multi-region).

6. Security

All data the app sends (to Firebase, RevenueCat and AdMob) is encrypted in transit using TLS. Firebase encrypts stored data at rest. Access to your cloud save is restricted by security rules to your own signed-in account, and purchase records can be created only by our server. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Retention

  • Guest data: on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Account and cloud save: until you delete your account. Deletion removes your cloud save, your purchase grant records and your sign-in account immediately.
  • Server logs: our Cloud Functions write operational logs (for example a user id and order id when a purchase is delivered) to Google Cloud Logging, which keeps them for about 30 days.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play: purchase records are kept by these providers as needed for billing, tax and fraud prevention, under their own retention policies.
  • AdMob: retention is set by Google's policies.

8. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account and data. You will be asked to choose your Google account again to confirm.
  • On the web: https://pourly-app.web.app/delete-account (sign in with the same Google account and confirm).

Both delete your cloud save, your purchase grant records and your Pourly sign-in. Deletion cannot be undone. Purchases themselves are handled by Google Play: Remove Ads and VIP stay with your Google Play account and can be restored, and subscriptions must be cancelled in Google Play (deleting your account does not cancel them).

Never signed in? We hold no data about you on our servers; uninstall Pourly to remove the progress stored on your device. For any other request (for example about purchase records held by RevenueCat for an anonymous id), email us.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing).

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): you may exercise the rights above and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states: you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Ads served by Google AdMob may count as "sharing"; you can opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings and, where shown, in Settings → Ad privacy options. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise a right, email rezahamza5@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder (for example by asking you to sign in). We reply within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows).

10. Children

Pourly is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to a PG content rating. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
