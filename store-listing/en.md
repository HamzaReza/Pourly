# Pourly: Google Play store listing, English (en-US)

Character counts are Unicode code points (what the Play Console counts).

## Title (25/30 characters)

Pourly: Water Sort Puzzle

## Short description (75/80 characters)

Pour and sort colors into tubes. 300+ levels, a daily puzzle and no timers.

## Full description (1800/4000 characters)

Pourly is a relaxing water sort puzzle. Tap a tube, tap another, and pour. You can only pour onto the same color or into an empty tube. The goal: every tube holds a single color.

Easy to learn, hard to put down. There are no timers and no pressure: take each move at your own pace.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap where to pour.
• Pour only onto the same color or into an empty tube.
• Sort every color into its own tube to clear the level.
• Stuck? Undo, use a hint, add an extra tube, or restart instantly.

300+ LEVELS
• More than 300 levels, each checked by a solver so it can always be solved, then endless new levels after that.
• From 3 colors up to 14 colors and 16 tubes.
• A hard level every 10 levels, marked with a crown, for double coins.
• Mystery "?" segments: some colors stay hidden until you pour away what sits above them.
• Earn up to 3 stars per level for solving it in few moves.

DAILY PUZZLE AND REWARDS
• A new daily puzzle every day. Solve it to keep your streak going.
• A calendar with your current and longest streak.
• A 7-day daily gift with a special chest on day 7.
• Unlock colorful themes for your tubes and backgrounds.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Color-blind mode: every color also gets its own symbol.
• Reduced motion option.
• Play offline, anytime.
• Optional Google sign-in to back up your progress and continue on another device.
• Available in 13 languages.

Pourly is free to play. It contains ads, including optional rewarded videos you can watch for hints, coins and more. In-app purchases are available: coin packs, Remove Ads (removes the ads shown between levels), a one-time Starter Pack and an optional VIP subscription (weekly or monthly, renews automatically until cancelled in Google Play).

Relax, pour and sort. How far can you go?
